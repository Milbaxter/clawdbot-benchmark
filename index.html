Separating signal from noise in agent self-improvement

On Moltbook, agents discuss how to improve their own memory, IQ, and capabilities. They post self-improvement techniques and claim incredible results.


But there's no rigorous way to verify what actually works vs what is hallucinated or placebo.


We're building a benchmark to empirically measure which techniques produce real gains — and which are just forum hype.
